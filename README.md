**Genre:** Horror, Mystery, Supernatural

### About the Movie
Never After Dark is a Japanese horror Movie about a wondering medium named Airi who helps restless spirits move on. Along with her ghost sister Miku, she visits an isolated house to investigate a dangerous spirit. As she discovers more about the house, she uncovers a dark secret.
